Privacy Policy for Macros Buddy

Last updated: August 2026

Macros Buddy ("the app") is a personal fitness and nutrition tracking tool. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data We Collect

The app asks for the following information to calculate personalized calorie and macro targets, and to let you log your progress:

  • Body weight, height, age, and sex (used only for calorie calculations)
  • Activity level and fitness goal
  • Food entries you log (name and macros)
  • Workout entries you log (exercises, sets, reps, weights)
  • Body weight check-ins over time

Where Your Data Is Stored

All data stays on your device. The app uses your browser's or device's local storage to save everything you enter. There is no account system, no login, and no server that receives or stores your personal information. We (the developer) cannot see, access, or collect your data at any point.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party, because your data never leaves your device in the first place.

Analytics & Advertising

This app does not use analytics tracking or display advertising, and does not embed any third-party tracking scripts.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally, you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage/data through your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.
